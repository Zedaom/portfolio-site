My Journey

Hey, I'm Moacyr Neto. Motion Designer and 2D Animator based in São Paulo, working in this field since 2017.

Over the years I got to work with brands like Huawei, Tiger Beer, The Ring Magazine, Riot Forge and Havaianas, and collaborated with studios like Split Studios, Colossal, The Youth, Envar Entertainment and Histeria Studio.

Rhythm, shapes and colors are what drive me. I'm always looking for new ways to express that through motion.
